Research

Understanding behavior, locating mechanisms, and improving safety

The work connects controlled studies of what models do with analysis of the internal computations involved, then asks how those insights change when models interact as part of a larger system.

Current direction

What I am working on now

The current research extends from individual-model behavior toward the social and system-level dynamics of interacting agents, while retaining a mechanistic lens.

01

Silent influence

When minimal context from one agent changes another model's behavior without appearing in its stated explanation.

02

Alignment mechanisms

How preference alignment and safety-relevant behavior appear in activation space and across transformer depth.

03

Interacting agents

How to study influence, attribution, coordination, and dominance in collaborative AI systems.

04

Mechanistic safeguards

How internal evidence can inform interventions and oversight rather than stopping at behavioral detection.
